Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

At AutoResponder Pro, our users' privacy is our number one priority. This application has been designed under the "Privacy by Design" principle.

1. Data Collection

Our application processes most information locally on your terminal. However, to guarantee the integrity of the free credit system, we use your device's unique identifier (ANDROID_ID). This identifier is securely transmitted and stored in Firebase Realtime Database (a Google service) solely to keep track of the 30 trial messages and prevent fraud through reinstallation.

We DO NOT collect or transmit: contact names, the content of your personal messages, or any other private information from your conversations.

2. User Legal Responsibility

By using this tool, the user acknowledges being the solely responsible for the processing of personal data of third parties in accordance with the legislation in force in their country (GDPR or others). AutoResponder Pro does not act as a data processor for the content of your messages, as it has no technical access to them.

3. Third Party Services

We use Firebase (Google Cloud) exclusively for the technical management of the usage counter and license verification. This data is protected under Google's security standards.

4. Android Permissions

  • Notification Access: Required exclusively to read WhatsApp messages and be able to respond automatically.
  • Contacts: Used to identify the phone number of customers and to manage the Blacklist. Selected names from your agenda never leave your device.
  • Battery: Requested to prevent the system from stopping the background response service.

5. Link and Content Processing

Any link (website, catalog, social media) or additional text configured in the application is stored only in your phone's private storage. AutoResponder Pro does not track clicks or the use your customers make of such links.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, you can write to us at appsaiber@gmail.com.
