Last updated: July 31, 2026

At AutoResponder Pro, our users' privacy is our number one priority. This application has been designed under the "Privacy by Design" principle.

Our application has been designed to guarantee maximum privacy. AutoResponder Pro DOES NOT collect, DOES NOT store, and DOES NOT share any type of personal information, device identifiers, or the content of your conversations outside your terminal.

All message processing and response logic occurs exclusively locally on your mobile device. No data is transmitted to external servers.

3. Third Party Services

This application does not use third-party data processing services. As there is no cloud database or external tracking systems, your privacy is technically guaranteed by the absence of external data connections.
